Drafting toolsS-101

The drafting tools you already know.

Type commands or click them. Every tool is a command, so the menus, the command line, scripts and the assistant all do the same thing.

01 · COMMAND LINE

Type it or click it

LINE, CIRCLE, TRIM, OFFSET, FILLET, ARRAY and the rest, with prompts, keywords, @dx,dy and direct distance entry.

02 · PRECISION

Snaps and tracking

Endpoint, midpoint, centre, intersection, tangent and more. Polar tracking, ortho and a grid for exact geometry.

03 · ORGANISE

Layers and blocks

Layers with colour, linetype and lineweight. Blocks with attributes, tables and schedules.

04 · ANNOTATE

Dimensions and hatches

Linear, aligned, radius, diameter and angular dimensions, leaders, text, and hatch patterns of our own.

05 · FILES

Your drawings open

Opens DXF and DWG drawings, saves DXF, exports PDF, SVG and PNG. Layouts, viewports and plotting.

06 · AUTOMATE

LISP and scripts

Run AutoLISP-style routines and script files. Drive the app from other programs over a control channel.

Drawing togetherS-201

Share a link.
Draw at the same time.

Like a shared document, but for drawings. Everyone sees each other's cursors and edits as they happen, from the desktop app or the browser.

  1. ShareFile ▸ Collaborate… gives you a link. Send it to a friend.
  2. JoinThey open the link in the browser or the app. No account.
  3. See each otherNamed cursors, who is selecting what, and Follow to watch someone's view.
  4. Keep itThe drawing stays on the server. Open it again from your cloud files at any time.

Tell it what you need.
It works on your drawing.

The assistant reads the open drawing, runs the same commands you would, and looks at the result before it answers.

  1. Reads the drawingCounts, sizes, layers, text and blocks. “How many M8 holes are on this sheet?”
  2. Draws and editsParts from a description, dimensions, layer clean-up, title blocks, schedules from blocks.
  3. Asks youIt can ask a question, or ask you to pick points or objects on the drawing.
  4. Asks firstSaving, opening files, exporting, purging, big erases and LISP wait for your Allow.
  5. One undoEach request is a single undo step, so a wrong turn costs one Ctrl+Z.
